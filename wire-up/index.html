How to use Wire-Up

        
    
          
  • Add parts: drag from the toolbox, or click an item to drop it in the middle of the view.
    • 
          
  • Wire: drag from a breadboard hole or a part leg to another one. Wire mode (W) lets you start anywhere.
    • 
          
  • Move: drag a part. Drag a wire end to re-plug it. Drag battery leads and meter probe tips onto the circuit.
    • 
          
  • Pan / zoom: drag empty space with the left button (or the middle button, or hold Space and drag). Mouse wheel zooms.
    • 
          
  • Select several: left-drag a box on empty canvas (it must cover each part fully). Ctrl+click or Ctrl+drag adds to the selection, Ctrl+A selects all. Left-drag one of them to move the lot; arrow keys nudge a step.
    • 
          
  • Copy: Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V to paste, Ctrl+D to duplicate.
    • 
          
  • Side panels: the arrow tabs at the screen edges (or [ and ]) hide the toolbox and the inspector.
    • 
          
  • Wires keep out of parts: a new wire routes around the parts in its way, and dragging a corner or end across a part makes the wire wrap round it (a corner cannot be dropped on a part). Wires can still run over the breadboard.
    • 
          
  • Branch a wire: drop a wire end on the middle of another wire and it joins it, takes its colour, and goes away if you delete the part it was pulled from.
    • 
          
  • Wires follow the grid in right angles and route around each other. Drag from a hole even if a wire passes over it; click the wire to select it.
    • 
          
  • Rotate: R. Replace burnt parts: Y. Delete: Del. Undo / redo: Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y. Fit view: F.
    • 
          
  • Tweak values: select a part and use the inspector, or Shift + mouse wheel over a part.
    • 
          
  • Code: the Code button in the top bar opens the code window of the Arduino boards. Click a board to see its name and the sketch it holds; Upload flashes the text into that board.
    • 
          
  • Click switches to flip them, hold the mouse on a push button to press it, click the meter dial to change mode.
    • 
          
  • Parts burn when you push them past their rating. Select a burnt part and press Replace.
